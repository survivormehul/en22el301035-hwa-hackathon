Good to know

Questions, answered

Is this a one-off box or a subscription?

Every box is a subscription. You choose monthly or yearly billing, and you can pause, resume or cancel from your account.

Can I change my box or billing frequency later?

You can change quantity, dog assignment and delivery preferences. The box type and billing frequency stay locked after purchase to keep billing clear; yearly subscriptions can skip an upcoming delivery.

How is shipping calculated?

Shipping is based on your delivery pincode with metro, standard and remote zones. The exact amount is shown at checkout before the order is placed.

Are payments, OTPs and emails real?

No. This is a demo storefront. Verification codes are shown on screen, emails are recorded as simulated notifications, and every order is created with payment pending. No real charge is made.

Do you offer coupons?

Yes - a first-order discount coupon and a free-delivery coupon are available at checkout. Both are demo coupons and one can be used at a time.
